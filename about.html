About the practice

Revenue diagnosis before another growth prescription.

Kartik Clarity™ is a founder-led revenue diagnostic practice focused on helping B2B teams examine where pipeline, sales execution, follow-up, handoffs and retention may be breaking down.

The work is built around one principle: diagnose before prescribe. More leads, more software or more activity are not automatically the answer when the underlying issue may sit inside the revenue system.

How the diagnosis works

The Revenue Leak Architecture™ framework examines five areas:

  • Silent Zone: opportunities or signals that go unnoticed.
  • Trust Gap: friction that prevents buyers from moving forward with confidence.
  • Follow-Up Graveyard: conversations that stall without a clear next step.
  • Handoff Fracture: information or ownership lost between teams.
  • Renewal Blind Spot: risks to retention or expansion that are not surfaced early enough.

Evidence over impressive-sounding claims

Kartik brings 18 years of sales experience to this work. The diagnostic approach distinguishes observed facts from hypotheses and unknowns. Estimates remain estimates until they can be checked against relevant business data.

Transparency: examples shown in tools or walkthroughs may use illustrative sample data. They are not proof of a customer's results, and no specific revenue outcome is guaranteed.

Who this is for

The primary focus is B2B SaaS companies with $5M–$50M in annual recurring revenue (ARR) that want to inspect revenue friction before committing to another growth initiative.
