Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Kartik Clarity™ respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect through this website and our tools, why we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact information: name, work email, company, role, and information you voluntarily provide when contacting us, requesting a diagnostic, or expressing interest in an offering.
  • Business information: information you choose to enter into assessments, forms, diagnostics, or consulting workflows, including revenue, pipeline, conversion, sales-process, CRM, or operational information.
  • Transaction information: information associated with purchases or payment requests. Payment-card details are handled by the applicable payment provider rather than stored by this website.
  • Technical information: browser, device, IP address, approximate location, pages visited, referring pages, and similar information generated through hosting, security, analytics, or website infrastructure.
  • Communications: messages, emails, support requests, and other information you send to us.

2. How we use information

We may use information to operate and secure the website, provide requested tools and services, deliver diagnostics or consulting, process purchases, respond to inquiries, communicate about services, improve products and content, prevent misuse, maintain records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Diagnostic and AI-assisted processing

If you submit business information to a diagnostic or tool, that information may be processed to generate a directional analysis, score, classification, or recommendation. Where AI-assisted processing is used, outputs are estimates and should be reviewed by qualified humans before material business decisions or external use. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share.

4. Payments and third-party services

Purchases may be processed through Dodo Payments. Other infrastructure or service providers may process information on our behalf for hosting, authentication, communications, analytics, security, or application delivery.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for functionality, security, preferences, or analytics. Where consent is legally required, applicable consent choices will be provided.

6. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business needs, dispute resolution, security, and applicable legal or accounting requirements.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Requests may be sent to info.kartikclarity@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you are located. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we intend to use appropriate safeguards.

10. Children

Our services are intended for business users and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for these services.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this website with a revised “Last updated” date.

12. Contact
