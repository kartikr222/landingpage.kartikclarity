The problem

You're not losing deals. You're losing them quietly.

None of these show up as a single bad number. They show up as a slow, steady drift between what your pipeline should produce and what it actually does.

Leads go quiet after the first call.
Not because they said no. Because no one followed up in a way that earned a response.
Proposals stall for weeks with no reason given.
Trust broke down somewhere between the pitch and the paperwork, and nobody noticed when it happened.
Deals close, then the client goes cold.
The handoff from sales to delivery lost context, and the client felt like they were starting over.
Renewals surprise you, in the wrong direction.
By the time a client mentions leaving, the decision was already made weeks earlier.
