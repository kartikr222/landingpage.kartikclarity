How the work stays evidence-led

  1. Establish the evidence: review the available definitions, funnel data and operating process.
  2. Separate fact from hypothesis: label what the evidence supports and what still needs validation.
  3. Prioritize the mechanism: focus on the friction that matters, not vanity metrics or an assumed ROI.
  4. Verify after action: define what to measure to determine whether the suspected leak actually changed.
Transparency: Kartik Clarity™ does not claim customer outcomes or testimonials that have not been documented. Estimates depend on the completeness and quality of the evidence supplied. No specific revenue result is guaranteed.
